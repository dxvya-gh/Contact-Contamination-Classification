How is estimated risk calculated?

Deterministic Contact-Decay Algorithm • Model v1.0

A. Contact Detection

The ESP32 samples capacitive touch electrodes on GPIO12 and GPIO14 approximately every 100 ms. A confirmed contact event begins when raw capacitive readings fall below calibrated baseline thresholds with confirmation/debounce filtering to reject transient electrical noise. When a zone activates, a 300 ms classification window is evaluated before finalizing the event.

B. Event Weights & Hold Duration Scoring

  • Confirmed Single-Zone Contact: +4 provisional points at contact onset (doubled from +2).
  • Confirmed Dual-Zone Contact: +6 provisional points at contact onset (doubled from +3).
  • Hold Duration Scoring: Adds +2 points for each completed 5-second interval of uninterrupted contact (capped at +10 pts max). Points are awarded once as each discrete 5s interval is crossed and cannot be re-awarded.
  • Unified Dual-Zone Hold: When both zones are held simultaneously, hold intervals are evaluated on the unified contact session without double-counting between zones.
  • Dual-Zone Classification Window: Evaluates simultaneous contacts within a 300 ms window as a single dual-zone event (+6 pts), never as two single touches plus a dual touch.

C. Time Decay Formula

R(t) = R(previous) × exp(-Δt / τeffective)

Elapsed time gradually reduces the modeled risk score. The model uses a base persistence timescale (τbase) of 30 minutes, calculated on monotonic elapsed time rather than loop iterations.

D. Humidity & Environmental Persistence Modifiers

Valid readings from the SHT31 sensor modulate the effective persistence timescale (τeffective = τbase × Mhum × Mtemp), affecting how quickly risk decays over time:

Relative Humidity (RH) Modifier (Mhum) Model Heuristic Effect
< 35% (Dry) 0.80× Accelerates decay / shorter persistence (dry desiccation)
35% – 65% (Normal) 1.00× Neutral indoor baseline timescale
65% – 80% (Humid) 1.20× Slows decay / longer persistence (droplet retention)
> 80% (Very Humid) 1.35× Maximum bounded persistence multiplier
Invalid / Missing 1.00× Documented safe neutral fallback

Temperature Modifier (Mtemp): Centered at 22°C with +0.01/°C, clamped between 0.85× and 1.15×. The combined environmental modifier is bounded within 0.70× to 1.50×.

E. Score Limits & Risk Bands

The score is bounded strictly between 0 and 100 and maps directly to four risk levels:

0–24: Low 25–49: Elevated 50–74: High 75–100: Critical

When the score crosses the configured threshold of 75, an active sanitization recommendation alert is displayed.

F. Sanitization Reset

Activating “Mark as Sanitized” records an operator-reported cleaning action with a timestamp, archives the pre-reset score, and resets the authoritative model score to 0.0.

G. Scientific Limitations

“This is an experimental relative-risk estimate based on contact activity and environmental conditions. Capacitive touch sensors and temperature/humidity measurements do not detect bacteria or viruses. The weights and environmental model require validation.”
